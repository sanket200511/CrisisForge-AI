4 Core Intelligence Engines
01 — Inflow Forecast MC-P10/90
  • Trend + weekly harmonic sine (5 sin(2 pi t / 7))
  • Epidemic S-curve & trauma impulse surge dynamics
  • 200 Monte Carlo paths $ o$ P10–P90 uncertainty
02 — Resource Allocation HEURISTIC
  • FCFS arrival queue vs. Clinical Severity sorting
  • Demographic Equity quotas (Pediatric/Adult/Senior)
  • Greedy Acuity-to-Cost heuristic (Acuity Gain / Cost)
03 — ML Patient Triage GBM-SENS
  • GradientBoostingClassifier $ o$ 4 triage classes
  • GradientBoostingRegressor $ o$ stay duration hours
  • Empirical baseline perturbation attribution
04 — Transfer Balancing MULTI-RES
  • Composite Strain S_h (Beds 25%, ICU 35%, Vent 25%, Staff 15%)
  • Triggered at S_h > 75%; preserves 25% surge buffer
  • Distance-penalized matching: Headroom minus 0.5 * dist
